# App edit ops (content editing)

October 15, 2026. `src/app/ops/edit.rs` and `src/app/ops/edit/` port engine.ts's `edit-targets`, `edit-text`, `edit-paragraph`, `text-add`, `edit-replace-preview` and `edit-replace-apply` (with content-edit.ts) onto `AppSession` (stage A). Stage B adds `edit-object`, `edit-objects`, `clip-copy`, `clip-paste` and `edit-batch` on the same scanner and writer; the redaction group builds on both.

## Layout

- `edit.rs`: the six ops, limits, `Budget`, `decode`, the scan cache (`scan_for`), `resolve_target` (engine.ts `resolveEditTarget`), the replace preview.
- `edit/lex.rs`: the content tokenizer with byte spans (content-edit.ts `nextToken`), `next_q_end`. The crate's `content::ContentParser` keeps one span per operation, not per operand, and reads inline images and malformed input by its own stricter rules, so the scanner has its own tokenizer with engine.ts's rules.
- `edit/scan.rs`: `PageScan`, `Record` and its parts, ids, the structured-text association (`runs_of`, `match_runs`), growth limits, move rules, struct-tree lookup.
- `edit/walk.rs`: `scan_page` (`scanEditTargets`, `walkEditContent`): the bounded walk that builds the records.
- `edit/font.rs`: font facts (`editResolveFont`), the `Codec` of a simple font, proven code maps (`Cid`: Identity-H and coded), /ToUnicode and /W parsers, repertoires, WinAnsi tables.
- `edit/glyph.rs`: Quartz glyph blocks and their collapse into glyph lines.
- `edit/paragraph.rs`: paragraph discovery and the paragraph plan.
- `edit/plan.rs`: text plans (`editPlanText`, geometry, width rules, glyph-line plan), number and operand writers, `Measure`.
- `edit/write.rs`: `splice`, `privatise` (copy-on-write), `write_element`, `write_plans`, text-add's write.
- `edit/geometry.rs`: matrices, `edit_number` (`editNumber`), `round2`, JavaScript `Math.round`.
- `view/measure.rs`: `ViewDocument::page_transform`, `measurer` (codes measured with a page's fonts by hayro) and `decoded_stream` (hayro's tolerant decode of one stream).
- Shared files: `ops/mod.rs` routes the six ops; `AppDocument` gains the private scan cache (`edit_scan()`) and `view_with_document()`; `view/mod.rs` declares `measure`.

## Scanner

- One bounded pass per page, two walks as engine.ts does (a count-only walk refuses a page past 512 targets before structured text is read). Budgets: 2 MiB of decoded streams (CMaps and copy-on-write copies included), 128 streams, Form depth 16, nesting 32, 200k tokens, 4096 raw and 512 final targets, 4096 UTF-16 units per text and 65536 per reply, 256 pending operands.
- A `Record` holds the operator and operand spans of its stream, the stream object, the path (`c<i>` or `c<i>/<Name>@<Do offset>`), the text and font facts, the text-space state at the show (Tf, Tc, Tw, Tz, Tr, TL, the linear part of Tm x CTM x page transform, the origin), tail and paragraph links, copy-on-write needs, and for an image or Form the placement spans (`q`, the clip's `re` operands, `cm`), boxes and orientation. Items in the reply are built from records.
- Ids are engine.ts's: `p<page>.<path>.<operator offset>.<fnv1a32(op|payload)>`, `Do|<name>`, `GL|<key>`; paragraphs `para.<first id>`.
- Marked content: an inline /ActualText records the path of the stream holding it; a run takes it only from its own stream. A Form walk knows whether an ActualText (inline, from /Properties, or the StructElem of the enclosing page MCID) is open around its `Do`.
- The ParentTree lookup (`scan::parent_entry`) is done once per walk: depth first, each node once, a kid outside its /Limits not entered, at most 4096 nodes and depth 32.
- `scan_for` caches one scan per document, keyed on generation, revision, page and the document's content state (every write, undo, redo and abandon changes it), so a batch's nested writes never see a stale scan. `edit-targets` always scans afresh.
- Association and bounds come from the view's preserve-spans structured text (hayro), with quads and origins in single precision as MuPDF reports them.
- `PageScan::streams` keeps the decoded bytes of every walked stream; writers splice those when the strict decoder cannot read a stream the view reads.

## Measurement

- engine.ts measured a simple-font run with MuPDF's font handles from the structured text (`encodeCharacter`, `advanceGlyph`). Here the codes are shown with the run's own font (page or Form resources) through hayro's interpreter (`Measurer`): the advance is what the view draws (/Widths, else the program or the standard metrics) and glyph 0 means the font cannot draw the character. One interpreter cache serves a whole preview.
- Widths are page points: `x-scale x Th x (|Tf| x (sum(w)/1000 - TJ/1000) + Tc x n + Tw x spaces)`, x-scale being the length of the text x axis in page space. Identity-H and coded runs use their parsed /W or /Widths the same way.

## Writing

- Copy-on-write budget: each element's `privatise` is charged from the scan's decoded bytes (engine.ts: scan bytes plus that element's copies within 2 MiB), so a preview's verdict holds on apply for every element; `write_element`'s `copied` is the operation's running total of copied bytes, capped at `MAX_EDIT_COPY_BYTES` (16 MiB, never reached by content the scan accepts unless its raw streams are far larger than decoded). Pass one `privatised` set and one `copied` total for every element of an operation.
- Plans are complete before an operation opens; the write is one `mutate` step (`edit text`, `move text`, `edit paragraph`, `add text`, `replace text`).
- `write_element` privatises an element at most once per operation (`privatise`: inherited /Resources copied onto the page, a shared element copied with its raw bytes and filters, a depth-1 Form copied and registered as `<Name>c<n>` with this `Do` renamed), then splices it once with every plan and span (`splice` refuses overlaps), then writes StructElem /ActualText. Content is stored decoded; the app writer compresses it on save.
- For stage B and redaction: `write_element(tx, scan, page, element, plans, spans, privatised, budget)` takes raw `SpanEdit`s (a placement's `cm`, clip or `q..Q` span, a run's operand) next to text plans; `Record::placement` and `Record::object_ops` carry what edit-object needs; `plan::plan_text` with `Geometry` is edit-batch's text move; `Measure::glyphs` gives per-code advances for splitting a run at glyph boundaries; `PageScan::streams` has the bytes the spans index; `scan::runs_of` and `scan::match_runs` give a run's structured-text characters (quads) for glyph-level regions.
- Limits redaction must handle rather than assume away (a run it cannot place must be refused or removed whole, never skipped, or its text stays in the file):
  - `Measure::glyphs` measures one byte per code: it is None for 2-byte Type0 runs (use `Record::cid_write` widths, from /W and /DW, for Identity-H and coded runs) and a Type3 glyph's advance is None.
  - A run the structured text cannot match uniquely (two identical runs, `ambiguous-text-association`) has `bounds: null` and `match_runs` returns None for it.
  - `Record::origin` is set only for the first show on a line, and `text_linear` is the linear part alone; a tail show's position is the line start plus the advances of the shows before it (`prev_show` chain), which the scan does not compute.

## Differences from MuPDF and engine.ts

- Simple-font widths come from hayro's view of the font. MuPDF measured `Ä` in an unembedded WinAnsi Helvetica as 333 units (its Unicode lookup lands on another glyph); here it is 667, as drawn. The app test "every refusal token is reachable and none of them writes" expects `ALPHA` -> `BRÄVO` in fixtures/p0b/edit-transformed.pdf to fit; it is 84.02 pt against a 150.7 pt limit from x 72, so it is refused `overflow` like `BRAVO`.
- Simple-font advances are scaled by Th and the length of the text x axis; engine.ts multiplied em advances by the structured-text character size (the square root of |det| of the text matrix), wrong under Tz other than 100 or a non-uniform Tm or CTM. `Squeezed` in Helvetica 12 under `80 Tz` is 43.23 pt here (54.04 x 0.8) and 38.67 in engine.ts; under `2 0 0 1 72 700 Tm` 108.07 against 76.42. A line engine.ts accepted under Tz 80 can be refused `overflow`, and a size change's `shift` differs (-3.82 against -3.42).
- `aw ac string "` sets Tw and Tc for the runs after it (engine.ts left them unchanged, so a later run was measured without them and could overflow its limit).
- Character and word spacing count in text space through the text matrix (engine.ts added them as page points), and Identity-H and coded widths go through the text matrix (engine.ts used the Tf operand alone, 12 times too small under `12 0 0 12 Tm /F 1 Tf`).
- A move or size change keeps the run's own extent on the page. When the structured-text bounds start away from the run's first glyph (its proven origin past a leading TJ number; upright text) or off its line (its baseline raised by its Ts, which the scan tracks as `Record::rise`, a Form inheriting the rise of its `Do`), the structured text had no characters of its own for the run and its one match on the page is other characters (as for `(  ) Tj` spread by `17.75 Tc`): the checked box then starts at the first glyph, on the raised baseline when off the line (`plan::checked_bounds`); engine.ts checked the other characters' box with its smaller page-point spacing. A raised run's own bounds are on its raised line and are checked as engine.ts checks them. Corpus: govdocs1/000370.pdf (sha256 b78fa1c8d1db) page 1 refused every move here before (`outside-page`, a 432 pt box at x 540) and moves now, as on engine.ts.
- `unsupported-encoding` ("no glyph") is decided by code in the font the view draws with (glyph 0); engine.ts looked each character up by Unicode in the structured-text font, which fails for every character of an embedded subset TrueType font without a Unicode cmap. Runs in such fonts were refused even for a move, which keeps their codes (8 of 677 corpus files, e.g. govdocs1/000016.pdf sha256 58fe21359f and 000028.pdf sha256 99ec2ff852); here they move and edit when the font draws the codes.
- A run's old width counts a glyph the font lacks at its drawn advance; engine.ts counted it as 0.
- A simple-font run is measured without finding it in the structured text first; engine.ts refused `unmeasurable-width` when its text match failed (a run it could not associate is still not a candidate, so this only matters for a candidate whose line the structured text splits differently).
- `/MacRomanEncoding` and `/StandardEncoding` fonts decode and write through their own tables; engine.ts read them as WinAnsi and wrote printable ASCII only. Fonts without /Encoding keep engine.ts's rule.
- `true`, `false` and `null` are operands: a `BDC` dictionary with a boolean no longer refuses the page, and an inline image's `/IM true` is a 1-bit mask, so an unfiltered mask without /L is skipped instead of refused.
- Vertical writing is the CMap's: an `-V` encoding or a CMap stream with /WMode 1 refuses `vertical-writing`; the descendant's non-standard /WMode still counts, as in engine.ts.
- An inline /ActualText opened in another /Contents element than the run refuses the run `ambiguous-text-association`; engine.ts spliced the tag's span into the run's own stream at the tag's offsets, corrupting it. (Rewriting the tag in its own element in the same operation would also work; not done.)
- A run in a Form drawn under an ActualText open around the `Do` (inline, through /Properties, or the StructElem /ActualText of the enclosing page MCID) refuses `ambiguous-text-association`; engine.ts edited it and left the outer tag stale, so copy and search kept the old text.
- An inline /ActualText must decode (PDFDocEncoding or UTF-16) to the run's text for every font; engine.ts compared a simple font's raw bytes. The tag is written as printable ASCII or UTF-16BE for every font; engine.ts wrote a simple font's WinAnsi bytes (wrong for 0x80-0x9F as PDFDocEncoding).
- The ParentTree is read as a number tree (/Kids within /Limits); engine.ts read only the top /Nums, leaving a StructElem /ActualText stale.
- A /ToUnicode bfrange whose array destination never closes is parsed in linear time with the regex's result (engine.ts's regex backtracked quadratically).
- `edit-replace-preview` refuses `stale-target` when the request carries a generation or revision other than the session's (the renderer always sends its scan's); engine.ts ignored them (app-content-edit.md §7 #10), so a preview of a newer revision could list targets the renderer's scan does not show. Without the fields the preview answers as before; apply still relies on the token.
- Kept: image and Form item `bounds` are user space while text `bounds` are view space (§7 #9); renderer.ts mixes the two, so changing either breaks the reply contract. The "shared placement is rejected" comment and the dead `xobjectRefs` of §7 #10 have no counterpart here.
- Copy-on-write is charged per element from the scan's bytes, as engine.ts does; in addition one operation copies at most `MAX_EDIT_COPY_BYTES` in all (engine.ts had no total).
- Names written into content (`/<Name>c<n>`, a resized run's `Tf`) are `#xx`-escaped from their bytes; engine.ts wrote copy-on-write renames raw and escaped the UTF-8 of a Latin-1 name.
- A Form edited through copy-on-write is copied alone, with its stream's bytes and filters, and a copy of its /Resources dictionary when that is indirect; fonts and images stay shared. MuPDF's graft copied every object the Form reaches (fonts and their programs included).
- A content stream the strict decoder refuses (a bad zlib checksum) is read as the view reads it, and an edit writes it back decoded; the strict path alone would refuse the page.
- A page written directly into /Kids (not an indirect object) refuses edits (`invalid`); a StructElem written directly into the ParentTree array is not updated (`invalid`).
- `text-add` allocates a new font dictionary per add; MuPDF's font cache reused one object per base-14 font.
- Scan cache: one per document, keyed on the content state too; engine.ts kept one per engine and nulled it in clip-paste and edit-batch.

## Stage B: placements, clipboard, edit-batch

- `edit/object.rs`: `edit-object` (move, resize, delete, replace, duplicate, transform, crop) and `edit-objects`, with content-edit.ts's geometry (`page_clip_box`, `object_box`, `placement_matrix`, `rect_affine`, `transform_about`, `placement_after`, `plan_placement`, `check_full`, `placement_spans`, `duplicate_edit`). Every check before the step; the write is `write_element` with `SpanEdit`s (one privatise and splice per element, the operation's `privatised` set shared), step `edit object` / `edit objects`. Replace reads the image with `pages::images::read` (4 MiB file ceiling, stores at most `MAX_SAVE_BYTES`) and writes it with `images::store` (the annots group's identical change to images.rs, so the merge sees one edit), then registers `<name>r<n>` in the page's own /XObject after the privatise.
- `edit/clip.rs`: `Clipboard` (`app::Clipboard` re-exports it; the unit value `Clipboard` is an empty clipboard, as the misc group did for `CompareDocument`). `clip-copy` copies an image or Form XObject, or a run's font, with what it references, into a scratch store (`Scratch`: objects numbered 1.. with references renumbered inside it), one map per copy, so it outlives the document; the store and items sit behind one `Arc`, so another session of the engine (the harness's `clipboardFrom`) holds the same clipboard. `clip-paste` grafts them back with one map per paste (`Paster`), fresh direct /Resources with `PA<n>` and `PF<n>` entries, one new /Contents element, and engine.ts's in-step proof (a fresh scan finds every pasted object as a candidate at its box, every run once at its origin in the view's structured text); step `paste`.
- Field, link and mark members go through their own groups' code, so this group carries no copy of it: `fields`, `links` and `annots` listings at copy time (and for the occupied boxes and the new ids at paste time); a field through `fields::add_field(session, spec, value)` (engine.ts `addField` with its value override), a link through `link-add` dispatched by the router, each in batch `Plan` before the step (every check) and in batch `Run` inside it. A mark's rect is clamped and the combined mark cap checked here first, with engine.ts's verdict, then `redact-add` is dispatched in `Plan` as the check; the step writes the marks with `marks::write_plans`, their quads shifted with the rect (`redact-add` takes a rect only).
- `edit/batch.rs`: `edit-batch`. Run moves (`plan::plan_text` with `move`) and placement moves and deletes are planned against the one scan and written per element in one `write_element`; every other item is its single op through the router with only its `ALLOW` keys, first in batch `Plan`, then in batch `Run` inside the `edit batch` step (a nested `mutate` joins it). A refusal is tagged as engine.ts's `tagMember` does (an untagged one gets `stale-target`, or its reason when that is not `invalid`; the nested reply's extra fields are dropped) and always comes before the step, so the redo branch survives. `link-delete` names its link by /Annots object number: the `links` listing pairs it with the `linkId` the `link-delete` op takes (engine.ts called MuPDF's `deleteLink` with `linkPairs`). The batch mode is restored after every nested run.
- clip-paste picks a pasted field's name against the target's field tree with the forms group's bounded walk (`fields::field_tree_names`).
- Every number written into content (placement `cm` and clip operands, a pasted run's text state, a run's `Tf` and `Td`, a paragraph's operands, glyph-line blocks) is within `geometry::MAX_WRITTEN` (2^31 - 1, the ISO 32000 Annex C integer limit) or the operation refuses before its step.
- clip-paste and edit-batch check each member's permission (engine.ts `requireMutable` per member) once per distinct op, in member order, so the first refused member is the one engine.ts names; `detect` walks the document, and 512 members of one kind cost one walk.
- Rust tests: `tests/app_edit_objects.rs` (each action, edit-objects, the clipboard across sessions and documents, paste refusals, batch steps and refusals, hostile values, numbers past the limit, replace turns): every write is one step that undo and redo restore byte for byte, every refusal leaves the journal and bytes unchanged. Three tests drive other groups' ops (a batch of field-props, link-set and a move; a paste of an image, a field and a link; a pasted mark) and fail if the router does not know `fields`, `links` or `annots`.

### Differences from MuPDF and engine.ts (stage B)

- The page clip box (MediaBox within CropBox) inherits both boxes; engine.ts read the page's own keys, so an inherited MediaBox counted as Letter.
- A placement whose rect or box has no area (a Form with a zero-width /BBox) refuses move, resize, transform, crop, duplicate and paste as `unsupported-transform`; engine.ts wrote `Infinity` into its `cm`. Delete still works.
- A write whose numbers would pass 2^31 - 1 refuses: a placement (a near-zero /BBox drawn large, a huge `cm`) and a pasted run (a huge `TL` or `Tc` in its text state) as `unsupported-transform`, a run's size or move as `not-candidate` with reason `matrix`, a paragraph as `not-candidate`. engine.ts wrote JavaScript exponents (`1e+21`, which PDF has no syntax for: MuPDF reports an unknown keyword and draws with a garbage matrix) or `Infinity`. A pasted run's `Tr` goes through `editNumber` like its other numbers (engine.ts wrote it raw; the same for every integer mode).
- Replace reads PNG and JPEG only (MuPDF's `Image` also read other formats; the app's picker offers PNG and JPEG) and stores them as pdf-from-images does (PNG samples Flate-compressed, an opaque alpha channel dropped, JPEG embedded as is). Messages say "the app" where engine.ts said "mupdf".
- Replace applies a JPEG's EXIF orientation inside a simple placement (the `cm` turned within the same box, the refit using the turned aspect); MuPDF placed the stored pixels, so a phone photo came out sideways. A replace-only placement (no simple placement) keeps its `cm` and the stored pixels, as before.
- The turn is recorded on the stored image as the private key `/TPDF_Orientation <2..8>` (`pages::images::TURNED_KEY`), and an image page pdf-from-images makes from a turned photo carries it too. A replace takes the old image's recorded turn back out of the `cm` before refitting and turning for the new picture, so a photo replaced by an upright picture comes out upright (and refitted as an upright placement is), while a rotation or flip the user made is kept as engine.ts keeps it. Without the key a turned `cm` cannot be told from the user's rotation. The key is ignored by readers; a copy pasted or duplicated keeps it with its turned `cm`.
- A copied object never brings pages along: a reference to a page, a page-tree node or the catalog becomes null in the copy. MuPDF's `graftObject` copied what it reached, so a Form whose resources named a page pasted that page, its tree and its content into the target.
- clip-copy's 32 MiB ceiling stops storing stream bytes once passed (the copy is then refused, with engine.ts's verdict and member order); engine.ts grafted everything first.
- edit-batch's "not available" refusal names no reason in its message, as engine.ts's does (edit-object and edit-objects add ` (<reason>)`); the reason stays in the reply's `reason`.
- A copied mark keeps the quads `marks::page_marks` reads (page space, at most `MAX_MARK_QUADS` per mark and `MAX_DOCUMENT_QUADS` per page); a mark past either is copied as its rect, which is how region tests already treat it. engine.ts kept every quad.
- The mark cap counts Redact annotations with a four-number /Rect on every page (redact.ts `redactMarks` through MuPDF's annotation list).
- Kept: clip-paste ignores the request's generation and revision (§7 #10; a paste is not tied to a scan); replace keeps the old /XObject entry (it may be shared with other pages); a duplicate shares the resource name.

## App tests not passed by design or by the harness

- p3-edit "every refusal token is reachable and none of them writes": the `BRÄVO` width above (MuPDF measurement defect).
- p6-paragraph "§3.4 word-too-wide; ... undo restores": holds a page object of `session.doc` across `undo`; the harness's MuPDF copy is per revision, so the page shows the pre-undo state.
