# Acceptance review: edit-object, edit-objects, edit-batch, clip-copy, clip-paste (rounds 1 and 2)

October 16, 2026. Fixes for the acceptance review of these ops at ed62a16 (verdicts: edit-object, edit-objects, clip-copy accepted; edit-batch and clip-paste not accepted). Read with `../APP-ENGINE-DESIGN.md` and `app-edit.md` (corrected where these fixes change it).

## Pasted marks lose their quads (major, clip-paste; minor, clip-copy)

- **Verified.** `ClipItem::Mark` held the annots listing's rect only and the paste dispatched `redact-add` (a rect only), so a pasted search mark had no /QuadPoints: redact-apply removed its whole rect (both lines in the review's probe), the listing lost `search: true`. engine.ts copies `getQuadPoints` and pastes them shifted through `addRedactPlans` (§10.22, no search tag). The "difference" `app-edit.md` recorded was a shortcut, not a better behaviour.
- **Fix.** clip-copy reads the page's marks once (`marks::page_marks`, page space) and keeps each copied mark's quads in `ClipItem::Mark { rect, quads }`, matched by object number as the listing row is. clip-paste shifts them by the paste's (dx, dy) with the rect, still checks each mark as a `redact-add` plan before the step (permission, page, clamp; the combined cap first), and writes all marks inside the `paste` step with `marks::write_plans` (MuPDF's dictionary; the /Rect is the quads' bounds grown by a point, as `update()` leaves it). No search tag, as engine.ts.
- Measured with the review's probe (p7-markquads): the pasted mark's /Rect and quads equal MuPDF's (to float precision), the paste and listing replies are identical, and redact-apply removes `charlie` and `delta` on both pages and nothing else (engine.ts's apply refuses that document in its own text check).
- The pasted mark has no search tag (engine.ts: a pasted mark is not a search hit on its page, and the tag would make apply look for the text there), so apply takes its whole quads where a tagged mark's hit is its quads less the 2-point search margin (`region::hit_rects`): a space the margin reaches beside the words goes too. Never less than the copy removes.
- **Bound, recorded:** a mark past `MAX_MARK_QUADS` (1024) quads, or past `MAX_DOCUMENT_QUADS` on its page, is copied as its rect, which is how region tests already treat it (a superset, never less). engine.ts kept every quad.
- Test: `a_pasted_mark_keeps_its_quads_and_redacts_what_the_copied_one_does` (`tests/app_edit_objects.rs`): a two-quad mark over `charlie` (line 1) and `delta` (line 2) pasted onto the other page keeps both quads exactly and lists `search: true`, undo and redo exact; pasted onto its own page its quads step 10 points with the rect; redact-apply leaves `alpha bravo` and `echo foxtrot` on both pages and the two pages' text equal (before: page 1 lost both lines).

Measured on the corpus file: the six moves the review tried and its batch now succeed on both engines; for the (1, 1) move the replies, the saved page content and its pixels (0 differing) are identical.

## Round 2: a raised run checked at its baseline (major, edit-batch; edit-text too)

- **Verified.** Round 1's off-line branch assumed bounds off the run's line were other characters'. The scan did not track Ts, so a run raised past about 1.6 em (`72 745 Td 30 Ts (Superscript) Tj`, Helvetica 12) had its own bounds off the unraised line and was checked at its baseline: moves of 5, 12 and 20 points up near the top edge and a size change to 20 were accepted with the glyphs leaving the page (saved at y -7.9 to 8.6), and moves of 20 and 34 points down near the bottom edge that keep them on the page were refused, in edit-text and in edit-batch. engine.ts and ed62a16 agreed with each other on all of these.
- **Fix.** The scan tracks Ts (`TextState::rise`, saved with `q`, carried across /Contents elements, inherited by a Form from its `Do` as MuPDF's graphics state is) and records it as `Record::rise`. `checked_bounds` raises the origin by it through the text matrix (`rise * c`, `rise * d`) before the on-line test, and an off-line box sits on the raised baseline. A raised run's own bounds are on its line again, so it is checked as engine.ts checks it. The other-characters case (govdocs1/000370.pdf) is unchanged. The x-only alternative was not taken: it leaves a matched other characters' box on their line, not the run's.
- Matching is unchanged: the matcher's nearness test still uses the unraised origin, as engine.ts's `editMatchRuns` does.
- Tests: `a_raised_run_is_checked_where_its_glyphs_are` (edit-text moves and size changes and edit-batch near the top and bottom edges, engine.ts's verdicts and boxes, refusals writing nothing, accepted edits undo and redo exact) and `a_form_run_is_checked_with_the_rise_of_its_do` (`30 Ts` before `/Fm1 Do`). Both fail on fece880; the Form test also fails without the inheritance.
- Measured against engine.ts with the review's probes: q3-runbounds (14 synthetic pages: rises of 30, -25 and 14 near each edge, TJ leads, leading spaces, Tz, Tc spreads, a negative size) and q6-risebatch now give the ed62a16 verdicts on every request; the one remaining difference is the recorded Tz width (`Squeeze` under `50 Tz`, 23.68 pt here). The saved batch moves of the raised run land its glyphs where MuPDF's do (y 350.1 to 791.6 for the 34 point move).
- Corpus, against the fece880 and ed62a16 results: the edge sweep (381 files, 6,960 run moves 1 point inside and outside each page edge) changes no outcome (the same 240 recorded disagreements with engine.ts); the object, batch and clip differential (677 files) changes no file, govdocs1/000370.pdf still identical on both engines.
