October 15, 2026. Stage A: the four mark ops on `AppSession` and the mapping from marks to content that stage B (redact-apply: removal and sanitization) builds on. ## Layout

- `src/app/redact/marks.rs`: `Mark` (page, id, page-space rect and quads, search tag), `marks`/`page_marks`/`count` (redact.ts `redactMarks` over `annots::list`), `redact_rect` (`redactRect`), `Plan` and `write_plans` (`addRedactPlans`, generic over `Edit`), `set_rect` (`setRedactRect`), `search_plans` (`redactSearchPlan`), `SEARCH_MARGIN` (the two points a hit quad grows by).
- `src/app/redact/content.rs`: the content walk (`Walker`, `Content`, `Draw`, `DrawKind`, `TextDraw`, `Glyph`, `Detail`, `Limits`, `WalkError`), `content_streams`, `reachable_xobjects`, `count_alt`.
- `src/app/redact/fonts.rs`: `FontMetrics`, what the walk needs to place glyphs (code lengths, advances, box height); `FontCache` (CMaps and /W tables shared by a walk's fonts, and the work reading took).
- `src/app/redact/region.rs`: `Regions` (marks as page-space rects: `touches`, `contains`, `meets`, `touching`), `GlyphRule` and `hit_rects` (which glyphs apply removes), `page_content`, `page_annotations`/`annotations`, `appearance_streams`, `shared`.
- `src/app/redact/inventory.rs`: `Inventory` (redact.ts `redactInventory`), `info_entries`, `KEPT_INFO`, `STRUCT_KEYS`.
- `src/app/ops/redact.rs`: the four ops. Shared files: `ops/mod.rs` routes them, `app/mod.rs` declares `redact`, `AppDocument::view_unlayered` (document.rs, additive) with `layers::ShowAll` (what it redefines; acceptance fixes, `app-accept-redact.md`).

## Ops

- Validation in engine.ts's order with its messages and tokens; refusals come before the step, so nothing is journaled. Steps: `add redaction mark`, `mark redaction search hits`, `move redaction mark`. Replies: redact-add `{added: 1, pages: [page], ...journal}`, redact-search `{added, pages, ...journal}` (pages in first-hit order), redact-set `{...journal}` or `{noop: true, ...journal}`, redact-preview `{marks, pages, pageCounts, shared, sanitize, inlineAlt}` with no journal state.
- Dictionaries as MuPDF writes them: `/Type /Annot /Subtype /Redact /P /F 4 /Rect [/QuadPoints /PDF4AllSearch] /AP`, the annotation numbered before its appearance (a red outline, `appearance::redact`). An area mark keeps its rect exactly; a search mark's /Rect is its quads' bounds grown by a point (MuPDF `update()`). The search tag is a text string.
- redact-set: clamped rect (`detail: rect`), `findAnnotation` (`no-such-annot`), a Redact mark (`not-redact`), then the no-op test against `getRect` (`annots::page_rect`: /Rect less /RD) within 0.01, then /Rect, no /QuadPoints, no /PDF4AllSearch and a new appearance in one step. A moved search mark is an area mark; undo brings quads and tag back. Batch `Plan` and `Run` (edit-batch, clip-paste) go through `mutate` as elsewhere.
- redact-search: text 1..256 UTF-16 units without C0 controls or DEL; every page of `AppDocument::view_unlayered` (below) searched with `StructuredText::search_hits`; each hit one plan, each quad (single precision, as MuPDF's) grown by two points and clamped to the page box; `no-match`, then the 2,000-mark cap (`too-many`). The search stops one hit past the room left.
- The mark cap counts every Redact annotation of every page (`annots::list`, an object listed twice once).
- redact-add and redact-search (`write_plans`): a page whose /Annots is an indirect array other pages list too first gets a direct copy of it in the same step, so the mark lands on that page alone; the other pages keep the shared array. Since the annotations acceptance fixes (`app-accept-annots.md`) the copy is `annots::own_annots`, which `annots::append` runs for every new annotation, and a page written directly into /Kids gets its copy in its dictionary there (before, it kept the shared array).

## Region mapping (what stage B removes)

- **Regions.** A mark covers page-space rects: one per quad, else its rect (past 1,024 quads in a mark, or 262,144 in the document, the mark's union). Identical rects count once (Redact annotations sharing one /QuadPoints array can bring 262,144 copies of one quad). A page with more than 64 distinct rects indexes them in grids of 32, 16, 8, 4, 2 and 1 cells a side over their finite coordinates' range, each rect at the finest grid where it spans at most 16 cells (coordinates past the range, infinite ones included, count as its nearest cell, so every rect a query meets is among its candidates); a query visits the cells it spans in each grid (acceptance round 3: the 32 x 32 grid listed rects over more than 64 cells apart and cloned and sorted them for every query, 74-89 s of preview at 262,144 copies of one quad). `Regions::touches` is redact.ts `intersects` (positive overlap: paths, images, shadings, Forms, annotations); `contains` is a box inside one rect, closed, within 0.01 pt outwards (a box with no width or height included); `meets` is closed overlap (a point or a line counts); `touching` lists the rects a box touches.
- **Glyphs** (`GlyphRule::removes`, apply's rule and verify's; R1 of the redesign, October 18, owner decision D4). A glyph box is first cut to the page box: what lies past it is not seen, so a box wholly outside shrinks onto the nearest edge or corner and a mark reaching the edge takes what runs past it. It goes when (a) the cut box lies inside a mark's rect (every glyph under the black box, however small: 0.005 pt text, a zero advance, `0 Tf`, `0 Tz`, a singular `Tm`); (b) for an **area mark** (`Mark::by_area`: every mark without the search tag: redact-add, a region of redact-apply, a moved mark, a foreign mark with or without /QuadPoints, rect by rect), when the box itself (its quad, for turned text) meets the mark's rect less `AREA_TOLERANCE` (0.5 pt) on each side not on the page edge (`area_rects`; a rect thinner than 1 pt shrinks to its middle): everything a drawn box touches goes, a glyph cut by its edge included, while a box grazing the line above by less than half a point keeps its descenders; or (c) when its central half meets a mark's hit (MuPDF's text filter's central-half test): a search mark's quads less the two points the search grew them by (`hit_rects`), an area mark's rect too. So the neighbour a search mark's margin partly hides stays. A box with no position (NaN) goes when the page has a mark. A glyph whose width is under 0.1 em (a hostile /Widths 0, a combining mark) gets a box reaching 0.5 em either way from the pen (`INK_REACH`), so a box over its ink takes it. Before R1 only (a) and (c) held, so a glyph cut by an area mark's edge short of its middle stayed in the file under the box; a mark that lost its search tag now errs on the side of removing more. The view-text check keeps (a) and (c): the view's box for a font without metrics (Courier's descender) reaches past the walk's; the walk check enforces (b).
- **Unmeasured runs** go whole when `GlyphRule::reaches`: their box, cut to the page, meets a mark's rect.
- **Content walk** (`region::page_content(doc, view, page, Detail::Full, Limits::DEFAULT)`). One pass over the page's /Contents and every Form drawn, at any depth, tokenized with the edit group's lexer (byte spans). Each `Draw` names its stream object, the operator's span (`op`) and the operation's start (`start`: first operand; a path's first construction operator; an inline image's `BI`), the Form draw it is inside (`parent`, so each placement of a Form is its own chain), page-space bounds (unclipped: what a clip hides is still in the file), the clip's bounds, the innermost /MCID and whether an /ActualText, /Alt or /E property list is open (`alt`). `Content.streams` holds the decoded bytes the spans index; `Content.split` says an operation's operands began in an earlier /Contents stream (they are carried, as MuPDF reads the streams as one).
- **Text.** One `Glyph` per code: string operand (Tj, ', " operand index; TJ element index), code bytes within the decoded string, the pen's advance in unscaled text space (`w * Tfs + Tc [+ Tw]`; removing a glyph and keeping the rest in place is a TJ adjustment of `-advance * 1000 / Tfs`), page-space box: the advance across the font's height. Advances: simple fonts /Widths (/MissingWidth outside), else the view's (hayro) advances for the 256 codes, measured once per font; Type 0 the CMap (named ones from hayro's embedded CMaps, or the stream) and /W, /DW; Type 3 /Widths through /FontMatrix; vertical CMaps advance by /DW2 (unmeasured with /W2). Height: /Ascent, /Descent and /FontBBox where plausible, never inside 0.8 and -0.2 em (MuPDF's structured-text defaults), so a glyph box holds the view's character quad. Every render mode counts (invisible text is the classic leak).
- **Runs that cannot be placed** (unknown CMap, no advances, /W2): `measured: false`, no glyphs, one box holding every place the run could reach (each code at most the font's largest advance, plus spacing and adjustments, either way along the line). Later runs on the same line carry that `slack` and grow by it; Td, TD, Tm, T*, ' and " end it. Stage B must remove such a run whole when its box reaches a mark (`GlyphRule::reaches`), never skip it.
- **Paths.** Bounds of every point including control points; stroked, grown by half the line width times the miter limit (miter joins, clamped 1..10) or √2, times the CTM's largest stretch, at least half a point (hairlines). `W n` clips without a draw.
- **Images and shadings.** An image XObject or inline image draws its unit square through `matrix` (inverse it for the pixels under a region); `sh` fills the clip (the page without one). Inline images end at /L or /Length, else at the size an unfiltered image's /W /H /BPC and colour space give (`EI` after at most 64 bytes of white space), else at the first `EI` between white space that content follows (64 bytes of text or the stream's end), else at the first `EI` between white space. That search (`EiScan`) is kept per stream: data starts move forward, the next content-followed `EI` and the next non-text byte are cursors, and answers are kept for the next walk of the stream, so a stream is read about once however many images it holds (it was quadratic: 16,000 images without a content-followed `EI`, 352 KB, took 3.2 s; 50,000 now take 16 ms natively).
- **Forms.** A Form draw has its object, matrix and /BBox; its content's draws follow it with `parent` set. A Form already being drawn (self-invoking) is recorded with `entered: false` and not walked again.
- **Annotations.** `region::annotations` lists every /Annots entry with a /Rect that a region touches, Widgets, Links and Popups included, Redact marks excluded.
- **Limits** (`Limits::DEFAULT`): 8 million tokens (counted again for each Form drawn; each run of a stream also counts a token per 64 bytes, since a Form drawn again is lexed again whatever its token count; reading a font counts one, plus its /Widths or /W entries and one per 8 CMap bytes), 256 MiB of decoded streams, Form depth 32, 500,000 draws, 1,048,576 glyphs per walk; past them `WalkError::TooComplex`. Fonts are read once per walk: by object, a direct font dictionary by its address (it lives as long as the document), CMaps by name or stream and /W tables by descendant font through `FontCache` (a direct Type 0 font with 65,536 widths took 4 ms per `Tf`; set 20,000 times it now takes 8 to 16 ms in all). A stream neither the strict decoder nor the view can decode, or that does not tokenize, is `Unreadable`. Mismatched brackets drop the operand being read and the walk goes on (MuPDF's parser throws on the operand and resumes after the token); an operator inside an array or dictionary is still `Unreadable`. Nothing panics on hostile content (tests feed garbage, deep and exponential Forms).
- **Stage B** (built as planned, with what changed): see "redact-apply (stage B)".

## Preview

- `shared`: the draws of image and Form XObjects on marked pages (`Detail::Objects` walks). For each draw a mark touches, every other draw of the object that shows what the marks touch unmarked, once per marked page, object, other page and box:
  - an untouched draw: on a marked page, on another page whose resources can reach the object (walked), or in an annotation appearance (direct dictionaries in /Annots included; an annotation a region touches is left out, since apply removes it, Widgets included since stage B);
  - another touched draw whose page's marks do not cover the parts of the object this draw's marks touch: each part carried into object space and back out through the other draw's matrix must lie inside one rect there. A part is where apply changes the object: for an image, each rect's reach (past the page edges it lies on, as apply blanks pixels) within the draw's box; for a Form, per rect, the union of what apply changes inside it (any depth, below), each change cut to the rect's reach (acceptance fixes: the box decided it before, so a page-size /BBox drawn twice took the mark over one placement into the other). The other draw covers a part when, cut to its box and then to its page as the glyph rule cuts (a mark on that page's edge covers what lies past the edge, as apply's own reach there), it lies inside one rect there; a part without area (a glyph without width) counts where it lies in the box. A Form drawn alike on two pages, or twice on one page, with SECRETNAME marked at one placement and other text at the other is shared both ways; the same marks at every placement share nothing. Touched draws with the same parts in object space form a class compared once with every touched draw of the object, so 3,000 tiles of one image under one mark cost one comparison each.
- A Form draw is touched when apply changes something inside it, decided by apply's own tests (`form_parts`, acceptance round 2): a glyph `GlyphRule::removes_glyph` takes and a run that cannot be placed that `GlyphRule::reaches` (both cut the box to the page first, so text wholly past the page edge goes when a mark reaches that edge: its part is its own box, cut to the rect's reach, and another placement showing it is compared for it); a painted path, image or shading a rect touches (the rect's reach within its box); a clip a rect touches (apply cuts it), for the draws inside the same Form the cut then hides (the rect's reach within the clip and each such draw), so a page-size clip in a letterhead changes nothing where the Form draws nothing. Not its box: a letterhead with a page-size /BBox under a mark over body text is not touched, and apply does not change it; a glyph a mark only grazes (short of its middle) is kept, so it touches nothing. A marked page with a Form whose box a mark reaches (cut to the page: a Form drawn wholly past the edge included) is walked again as apply walks it (`Detail::Apply`, within the marked pages' token budget); when that walk fails, a draw inside it has no position, a Form was not entered (self-invoking), or the part work passes its budget, its box decides (reaching as above, its parts each rect's reach within the box). The part work is 16,777,216 units per marked page: each part found, once per Form it is inside; each glyph and clip read; each draw a cut clip may hide, once per rect the clip touches. Parts are kept up to 262,144 distinct (Form, rect) in the document. A clip is kept as one entry (its draw) until its rects are found, each charged, in the pass over what it hides. Memory is so bounded by the walk's draws plus the kept parts, whatever the clips and rects. (Round 2 recorded an entry per clip and rect, 72 bytes each, before checking the budget: the statement that the work passed 16,777,216 visits held for the visits but not for that list, which grew to 973 MB at 50,000 clips under 100 search hits and would pass wasm32's 4 GiB from a file of tens of KB; acceptance round 3.)
- A Form without a usable /BBox (missing, or without width or height, as `[0 0 420]` reads) has no bound in `shared`: the walk's stand-in for its box (the clip at that placement, or the page) does not hold what it draws past the page edge. Its box rule takes each meeting rect's whole reach, and other placements compare parts unclipped (re-review of round 3: past the part budget, such a Form lost text past page 1's edge from page 2, where it shows; preview said `shared` []). A shading (`sh`) inside Forms is compared where it may paint at every placement: within the usable /BBoxes of the Forms it is inside, unbounded without one, not the clip at this placement (found auditing the same class: a bbox-less Form's shading lost a band of page 2 right of its mark).
- Every rect a query of the glyph rule tests is counted (one counter per rule); a preview, an apply and its checks each refuse `scan-budget` past 134,217,728 (`MAX_QUERY_VISITS`), checked per glyph, per character and per draw, so one run of a million glyphs stops there too (second re-review: checked between draws only, it ran 13.7-25.3 s; 4,096 distinct tiny rects in one index cell under 100,000 zero-width glyphs made 409 million tests, 3.4 s, re-review of round 3).
- A page that can reach the object but cannot be walked counts as drawing it. When nothing is touched no other page is walked. At most 4,096 entries.
- Bounds: the marked pages' walks read at most 64 million tokens in all and keep at most 65,536 distinct touched draws (past either, `scan-budget`); other pages and appearances share 32 million units of work (walk tokens, XObject entries visited for reachability, /Annots entries read), past which what a page or appearance can reach counts as drawn (a page past it with any /Annots counts as drawing every touched object); reachability is kept per resources dictionary and each appearance stream is walked once; draws elsewhere are kept up to 4,096 distinct per object and 262,144 in all; pair comparisons stop at 4,194,304 mark rects carried (each part a draw's marks change, when found, and each part's query against each other draw, checked as each is made), past which a draw's parts are not found and a class counts as shown by every touched draw of its object (a singular matrix too). (Before round 3 the budget was checked only before a class's comparisons, and finding a draw's parts was not charged.) Stage A recomputed reachability per annotation per page (pages sharing an /Annots array of as many stamps whose appearance names 65,537 XObjects: 42 s at 200 by 200 in review; 300 by 300 now takes 0.1 s natively).
- A marked page past the walk's limits refuses the preview `scan-budget` (engine.ts's message); an unreadable one `invalid` ("the content of page N cannot be read (...)").
- `inlineAlt`: redact.ts's `/\/(?:ActualText|Alt|E)\b/` count over every stream the marked pages' walks read (their /Contents and the Forms they draw), each stream once per page.
- `sanitize`: `inventory::inventory`, walks bounded at 1,000,000 objects each (`too-large` past it).

## Differences from MuPDF and engine.ts

- redact-search reads a view with every optional content shown (`view_unlayered`, built from the cached view's own preparation: its resource budget, streams naming an external file read from their own bytes, image-filtered page content left out; then `layers::ShowAll`: the catalog without /OCProperties, every membership dictionary object as `<< /Type /OCMD >>`, a Form's own direct /OC dropped, in the private update), so text in a layer that /D or the layers panel hides, or that a membership shows only while a group is off (/AllOff, /AnyOff, /VE /Not), is marked; MuPDF searches what it draws. (Stage A only dropped /OCProperties, which turned every group on and so hid /AllOff and /AnyOff content MuPDF draws: the acceptance review's blocker.) A document with memberships but no /OCProperties is searched the same way (MuPDF draws all of it; hayro's view hid /AllOff content).
- redact-search has no per-page ceiling: MuPDF's search buffer holds 500 quads and engine.ts refused a page with exactly 500 hits (`too-many`). The 2,000-mark cap still applies.
- A page whose text the view refuses as too complex (1 MiB of shown bytes, 1,048,576 characters, self-invoking content) refuses redact-search (`scan-budget`, "the text of page N is too complex to search; mark it by area"); engine.ts's `attempt` read a failing page as no hits and left its occurrences unmarked. A document past the view's limits (its resource budget: "the pages' resources are larger than the view's limit") refuses `scan-budget` too (acceptance round 3; it was an untagged error).
- Hits are the view's (`app view` differences): one hit per match, one quad per run of adjacent characters; MuPDF splits a later match spanning lines into one hit per quad, so it writes more marks for such matches.
- A Redact annotation listed twice in /Annots is one mark (MuPDF lists and counts it twice); one without /Rect and /QuadPoints still counts, with MuPDF's empty rect.
- redact-set of a foreign mark with /RD keeps the /RD around the new rect (getRect is /Rect less /RD both ways), draws the outline in its /C, and writes the appearance as a new object (MuPDF rewrites its stream in place).
- `shared` counts draws at any depth (a Form in a Form, an image in a Form), any draw (not only `q cm Do Q` blocks), annotation appearances, and touch by unclipped bounds; engine.ts looked at depth-0 simple placements of indirect objects and their visible box. engine.ts scanned every page with the edit scanner (200,000 tokens, 2 MiB) and refused the whole preview when any page failed (`invalid`) or passed it (`scan-budget`), marked or not.
- `shared` also reports a touched draw whose page's marks leave uncovered what another draw's marks touch (engine.ts only reported untouched draws, so a Form marked in different places on two pages was not shared and per-placement copies would keep each page's unmarked part); counts appearances of Widgets a mark touches (apply keeps them); and refuses (`scan-budget`) past 64 million tokens on marked pages or 65,536 distinct touched draws.
- A marked page with mismatched brackets is read as MuPDF reads it (stage A refused it `invalid`; engine.ts previewed it).
- redact-add and redact-search give a page sharing its /Annots array with others its own copy before adding the mark; MuPDF and engine.ts append to the shared array, which marks the same rect on every page listing it (apply would then remove content there).
- Glyph removal (for stage B): a glyph wholly under the black box always goes, and a glyph partly under a search mark's two-point margin stays unless its central half meets the hit itself; MuPDF tests the central half against the inflated quads, so it also removes a neighbour (a space, or a letter after no space) whose central half reaches into the margin. Text past the page edge in the band of a mark reaching the edge goes too (it is not seen; a search hit running off the page was marked only up to the edge).
- `inlineAlt` also counts the Forms drawn on marked pages; engine.ts read their /Contents only.
- `sanitize.outlines` counts outline items (/First, /Last, /Next, /Prev); engine.ts counted every object with a string /Title reachable from /Outlines, through destinations into the pages. `sanitize.struct` counts dictionaries of the structure tree only (not entering pages, annotations, XObjects, fonts, streams; /Pg, /Obj, /Stm, /StmOwn not followed); engine.ts walked into everything the pages reach and refused past 50,000 objects (`too-large`), so a large tagged document could not be previewed. `annots` and `links` count each /Annots dictionary once per page (engine.ts: each entry, a non-dictionary one as an annotation). `attachments` walks the name tree with a cycle check and counts string keys (engine.ts's recursion overflowed on a cycle and reported 0). Each walk refuses past 1,000,000 objects.
- Reply numbers are doubles (as the annotation group's).

## Decisions (stage B)

- Widgets under a mark are removed with the redaction (and a field left without widgets, `fields::remove_widgets`), as other annotations are; MuPDF keeps them, value and appearance drawn over the black box. `shared` no longer counts the appearances of touched Widgets (apply removes them). The stage A test `shared_counts_direct_annotations_and_the_widgets_apply_keeps` became `..._and_skips_the_widgets_apply_removes` (deliberate change).
- redact-search still finds page content only (as MuPDF); text in annotations and field values is cleared by removal under a mark or by sanitize.
- `shared` decided touch by a draw's box (engine.ts's model); since the acceptance fixes a Form is touched by what it draws (Preview above), images still by their box.

## redact-apply (stage B)

### Layout

- `src/app/ops/redact.rs`: `apply` (the op: refusals in engine.ts's order, the copy, its checks, `check_copy` = `validateDocument(…, sanitized)`, `one_revision`), `shared_of` (preview and apply share it), `MAX_REDACT_SOURCE_BYTES`.
- `src/app/redact/apply.rs`: the removal on a scratch `Builder` (`apply`, `Applied`, `ApplyError`): the per-operation plan (`OpEdit`, merged over placements), stream rewrites (`show_replacement`, `path_splices`, `inline_replacement`, `shading_replacement`, `alt_splices`, `blank_comments`), `redact_images`, `clean_named`, `clean_structure`, `remove_annotations`.
- `src/app/redact/geometry.rs`: paths read from their operators (`parse`, `Subpath`, `Segment`), cut by convex regions (`Region`, `cut`, `Use::Area`/`Use::Lines`), written back (`write`, `number`).
- `src/app/redact/images.rs`: `Raster`, `read` (image XObjects: the strict decoder, else `ViewDocument::decoded_image` for DCT, JPX, JBIG2, CCITT), `read_inline`/`write_inline`, `unit_regions`/`pixel_regions`, `blank`, `encode`, `single_pixel`.
- `src/app/redact/sanitize.rs`: `Sanitize` (`checkSanitize`, `split` for `sanitized`/`kept`), `sanitize` (`sanitizeDocument`).
- `src/app/redact/verify.rs`: `content` (one walk of each rewritten page for the text check and the raw check), `view_text` (with redact-search's search of the copy), `search_left` (a copy with optional content), `pixels`, `marks_left`, `derived_left`, `searches`.

### Pipeline (engine.ts `redact-apply`, redact.ts `applyRedactions`)

- Refusals in engine.ts's order (amended by the redesign, below: a signed document refuses `signed` unless `unsign`; `shared-content` only as R3's safety net; `needs-flatten` for content only flattening can redact): `requireMutable` (edit permission), source over 50 MiB (`too-large`), the preview (`scan-budget`, `invalid`, the inventory's `too-large`), `no-marks`, `shared-content`, `checkSanitize` (`invalid`, `sanitize.<key> must be true or false`), the 12 MiB rewrite guard (`too-large`, "Redaction is limited to documents up to 12 MiB in this version"), the removal's refusals, `verify-failed` (text, pixels, raw, a mark left, the copy not one revision or not opening), output over 50 MiB, `validate`'s refusals. Every refusal carries the journal state; success carries none.
- The copy: a clone of the session's document (`Builder::from_document`): the session's document, journal, revision and marks never change (no `mutate`, nothing journaled: the scratch is not the session's document). The clone keeps the authenticated security handler, so `Encryption::Keep` re-encrypts as a save does; the encryption is the save's (`effective_security`: pending `none`/`aes-256`, else what the source had), with the request's entropy (the adapter now sends it for `redact-apply`).
- Then the removal (below) on every marked page, the inventory (after removal, as redact.ts), sanitize, the walk check, the write (`WriteOptions::APP`: reachable objects only, one xref section), the view, pixel and raw checks on the written bytes and the scratch (the raw check includes redact-search's own search of the copy), the marks and derived-copies checks, the strict reopen (one trailer), `validate` with `sanitized` (page count, encryption as the setting says, permissions).
- Reply `{bytes, marks, pages, sanitized, kept, verified: {text, pixels, raw}}` as engine.ts; `text` and `pixels` count every mark (any failure refuses), `raw` the distinct search texts checked.

### Removal

- **Plan.** Each marked page is walked (`Detail::Apply`) on the scratch. Every operation an edit reaches is keyed by its stream (a page's /Contents read as one, joined by newlines; or a Form) and span; a Form drawn more than once gets one edit merged from all its placements (glyph sets united, regions added), so it is rewritten once, in place (the preview refused any placement the marks do not cover). A show placed differently by two placements (a Form inheriting its font size) refuses.
- **Text.** Each glyph `GlyphRule::removes_glyph` takes (stage A's rule, the central half now the glyph's own quad, so turned text loses only what lies under the mark) leaves its show: the show becomes `[<kept> n <kept>] TJ` (hex strings), each run of removed glyphs and the adjustments among them one number, `-advance * 1000 / size` summed, so every kept glyph stays where it was (tested glyph by glyph). `'` becomes `T* […] TJ`, `"` `aw Tw ac Tc T* […] TJ`. A run the walk could not place goes whole when it reaches a mark, and nothing is shown in its place. At size 0 (`0 Tf`) an adjustment moves nothing but Tc and Tw still move the pen: the array is split and the run's move written as an adjustment at size 1 (`[…] TJ /F1 1 Tf [-n] TJ /F1 0 Tf […] TJ`) when the `Tf` name names the font in the stream's resources; a font an ExtGState set refuses. The rewrite walks the glyphs once (it was quadratic in a `TJ` array's length: 80,000 elements took 6.5 s natively).
- **Paths** a mark touches (by bounds): construction operators go and the paint operator becomes the cut path's (`geometry::cut` in the path's own space, the marks mapped through the inverse CTM): fills and clips keep the area outside the marks (a chain inside replaced by the region's border), strokes the lines outside (open subpaths cut at the border); `B`/`b` become a fill then a stroke; a clip is written after the paint. A path wholly under a mark goes; a clip wholly under one becomes empty (`0 0 m W n`). Curves are split exactly where they cross a region's edges (the parts outside stay curves, a part outside with a control point inside is flattened at 0.02 pt); a discarded path (`n`) a mark touches goes; a path with other operators inside it (a `cm`) goes. Lines and fills draw pixel for pixel as before outside the marks (tested with the view's rendering).
- **Images.** The pixels of each image XObject a mark touches are overwritten (the box's black in the image's colour space since R2, below; a stencil mask's "no paint") where the marks' quads, through the placement's matrix, overlap them, and the image is written back Flate-compressed (DCT, JPX, JBIG2 and CCITT decoded by the view's codecs; a JPX's own alpha becomes a soft mask). Its soft mask and mask are blanked the same way in new objects the image points to (another image may share them; the old one, unused, is not written). A placement wholly under a mark is no longer drawn (`Do` removed) and an image wholly blank becomes one blank pixel without masks. Inline images are rewritten (`/F [/AHx /Fl]`) or removed when wholly under a mark. An image that does not decode refuses; past 40 million pixels (`too-large`, "an image on page N exceeds 40000000 pixels", redact.ts's message) or 256 MiB of samples, refuses; one apply decodes at most 400 million pixels in all (declared sizes of images, masks and inline images; `too-large` past it).
- **Shadings** (`sh`) a mark touches are drawn inside `q … Q` with each region clipped out (an even-odd clip of the page around it, one per region); a function or mesh shading (types 1, 4 to 7) needs the page flattened (R4: their data is positional, so removing the `sh` alone would keep it in the file).
- **Content the walk does not follow** (`Planner::check_paint`, on every draw apply edits). A fill or stroke painting with a tiling pattern whose cell shows text, images (stencil masks included) or a function or mesh shading, or with a shading pattern of such a shading; a draw under a soft-mask group (ExtGState /SMask /G) that shows them; removed text in a Type 3 font whose glyph procedures draw text, image XObjects, non-stencil inline images or shadings: needs the page flattened (R4, `ApplyError::NeedsFlatten`; before the redesign it refused `invalid`). Cells, groups and procedures are walked (`Detail::Full`; nested patterns and groups to depth 8, each once; 32 million tokens in all, `too-large` past it); one that cannot be read refuses. A cell or group of paths and axial or radial shadings is a texture or a gradient: the paint under the mark is cut, the cell or group kept. Type 3 glyph shapes (paths, stencil bitmaps as TeX writes) stay with the font.
- **Derived copies.** A marked page loses /Thumb, /PieceInfo and /Metadata (a thumbnail is a raster of the page, Illustrator or InDesign /PieceInfo holds the original artwork, page XMP may quote it); a rewritten image loses /Metadata, /PieceInfo, /OPI and /Alternates, a rewritten Form /Metadata, /PieceInfo and /OPI.
- **Alternate texts.** A modified draw's (an image partly blanked included) marked-content sequences with /ActualText, /Alt or /E (and their enclosing ones, in its Form and around the Forms it is in) lose those entries: inline ones in the `BDC` operand, named ones in /Properties (the page's resources, or the node they inherit from, or the Form's). The structure elements of its MCIDs (the page's or the Form's /StructParents) lose them, and so do their ancestors up to the tree root.
- **Page content.** Each marked page gets one new /Contents stream: `q`, the old content (comments blanked, `Q` operators with nothing to restore dropped), what the content left open closed (`ET`, `EMC`, `Q`), then `q 0 g` and a box per mark rect (each quad's bounds, in user space) `f Q`. Streams are Flate-compressed.
- **Annotations.** Every mark goes, and every annotation a mark rect touches: Widgets through `fields::remove_widgets` (from /Annots, /Fields or the parent's /Kids, a parent left empty too, references cut; one write per page and parent and one of /Fields, each parent's kids leaving in turn as `remove_widget` one by one would: 32,000 widgets took 8.9 s natively, now about 0.2 s), others through `annots::remove_many` (with their Popups), dictionaries written into /Annots by position.
- **Structure.** The ParentTree is read once into a map (it was searched again for every MCID: 60,000 MCIDs, 9 s natively).
- What becomes unreachable (old content streams, removed annotations and fields, replaced appearances) is not written; the copy has one revision.

### Sanitize (redact.ts `sanitizeDocument`)

- After the removal, on the scratch, the nine toggles only (`REDACT_SANITIZE_KEYS`): `info` (every /Info entry but Producer, CreationDate, ModDate), `xmp` (the catalog's /Metadata), `attachments` (/Names /EmbeddedFiles, FileAttachment annotations), `outlines`, `annots` (every annotation but Widgets, Links and marks; Popups too), `links` (/URI, /F, /Win, /Mac and /Unix of each Link's action and the actions its /Next chain reaches, direct links too), `fields` (/XFA, /SigFlags; signature fields leave /Fields and the pages; every other widget and up to seven parents lose /V, /DV, /RV, /I, /AP, a button's /AS becomes Off, and the widget gets a new empty appearance through `forms::write::refresh`), `layers` (each OCG /Name becomes `Layer n`; /D and /Configs entries lose /Name), `struct` (/ActualText, /Alt, /E leave every dictionary of the structure tree `inventory` walks, and every property list named in /Properties of a page's resources or a Form they reach; `inventory` counts both, so `sanitized` and `kept` report them).
- `sanitized` is the inventory taken after the removal with the toggles off zeroed; `kept` the same with the toggles on zeroed (redact.ts).

### Checks

- **text**: the rewritten marked pages walked again (`Detail::Full`) hold no glyph `removes_glyph` takes and no unmeasured run that reaches a mark; and the copy's view (written bytes, or, when the copy keeps a stream naming an external file, a plain copy without /F that the view can read; every character drawn, clipped or not, `unclipped_blocks`) reads no character whose box lies inside a mark or whose centre lies in a mark's hit (`GlyphRule::under`; `removes` takes every glyph such a character's box sits in). A failure names the marks (redact.ts's JSON).
- **pixels**: the copy drawn by the view at 72 dpi, with annotations, is one colour in each of a mark's rects (each quad's bounds), two pixels in. Each distinct pixel rect is checked once, row by row: each row the rects cross is read once (where each pixel's run of one colour ends), and a rect is one step per row, one colour when its row's run from its left edge reaches its right edge and that colour is its first row's (acceptance round 3: every pixel of every rect of every mark was walked; 262,144 page-size quads took 290 s at 1,287 MB, 16,384 took 18.8 s). Past 134,217,728 rect rows in all, apply refuses `scan-budget` ("the marks are too many to verify against the drawing of page N"; before the final review the marks of the page failed, `verify-failed`), refused rather than passed.
- **raw**: each mark's search text, as UTF-8, UTF-16BE with and without its mark, or Latin-1, in the strings shown by `Tj`, `TJ` (each array's strings also read together), `'` and `"`, in /ActualText, /Alt and /E strings of property lists, and in comments, of every stream the marked pages draw or their resources reach (Forms), decoded; and redact-search's own search (`StructuredText::search_hits`) of the copy's marked pages, in the view the text check reads (or, when the copy has optional content, a plain copy with every optional content shown, `layers::ShowAll`, and external-file streams readable, as redact-search reads), so text in a CID font, split over several shows or in a hidden layer is found. A search mark moved off its text while another mark of the same search remains fails here ("raw check failed: a searched text is still in the marked pages' content"; the search text is not named, final review); a moved mark is an area mark (stage A, as engine.ts), so when the last mark of a search is moved away nothing is left to search for.
- **pixels** renders a page past the view's 2^26-pixel bound (or 65,535 pixels an edge) at the largest scale within it (stage B refused such a page `verify-failed`); the two pixels in are then more than two points. A page the view refuses for its drawing's cost at that scale (the render's scene and pixmap budgets, which grow with the pixels drawn) is drawn again at half and then a quarter of it; at a reduced scale every rect the full scale checks must keep two pixels each way inside its two-pixel margin, else apply refuses `scan-budget`, as it does when the page cannot be drawn at all (final review; `app-accept-redact.md`).
- **messages**: a `verify-failed` refusal names how many marks failed and on which pages ("text check failed: text is still under 2 marks on pages 1 and 3", "pixels check failed: something is still drawn over 1 mark on page 2"), never their rects or search texts: the window shows the message. Before the final review it carried the marks as JSON, search texts included; engine.ts (redact.ts) was changed the same way.
- No mark is left; no marked page keeps /Thumb, /PieceInfo or /Metadata; the written copy opens strictly (with its password) as one revision.

### Bounds

- The walks keep stage A's limits (`scan-budget` past them). Path cuts share 20 million segment visits per apply (`too-large` past it). Before any cut, the planned edits hold at most 1,048,576 copies of mark regions in all (a path's or shading's regions, an image's or inline image's quads; 64 bytes each; a shared path list's key, 32 bytes a rect, is charged a unit per rect too, so at most 64 MiB), `too-large` past it ("the paths under the marks are too complex"); paths drawn with the same matrix under the same rects of a page share one list, charged once with its key; a list with no region (rects without area make none) is not kept (second re-review of round 3: such keys were kept uncharged, 2,000 zero-width quads under fills each with its own matrix grew 62 KB a fill) (a page whose every run sits in its own page-size clip, searched for a common word, shares one list per page: 3 pages of 600 runs held 1.08 million copies and refused, re-review of round 3); a Form's path drawn at two placements under different rects holds both lists in one copy, charged; a path that goes whole holds none (acceptance round 3: 50,000 clips in a Form under 100 search hits held 320 MB of regions before the cut budget refused, and the walk's 500,000 draws under 2,000 marks would pass 4 GiB). Each cut costs at least one visit per region it holds, so the cut budget alone would refuse past 20 million; plans holding between 1,048,576 and that, with cuts that cheap, now refuse earlier. Each distinct black box is drawn once (262,144 copies of one quad made one path the view refuses to draw, and the pixel check failed). Image blanking visits at most 50 million region rows per image; images decode 400 million pixels in all per apply (80 inline images declaring 4000 by 4000 took 5.5 s natively, linearly, without a total bound). The marked pages' joined /Contents hold at most 256 MiB in all, a /Contents element listed again counted again, checked before they are joined (`too-large`; a 4 KiB stream listed 64,000 times, 388 KB of file, was joined to 262 MB and peaked at 1.8 GB). The pattern, group and Type 3 checks read 32 million tokens in all. The raw check reads at most 512 MiB of decoded content and visits a million XObject entries. Nothing allocates by a size a file declares before checking it against those bounds; nothing panics on hostile content (tests feed malformed operators, mixed paths, huge inline images, undecodable data, NaN and huge numbers, singular matrices).

### Differences from MuPDF and engine.ts (stage B)

- Line art is cut, not removed: MuPDF's `applyRedactions` with `REDACT_LINE_ART_REMOVE_IF_TOUCHED` (engine.ts's choice) drops every path whose bounds touch a mark, so a page-wide rule or background box under a small mark vanished; here only what lies inside the marks goes and the rest draws as before.
- Text keeps its neighbours: MuPDF tests glyphs against the search quads grown by two points, which reach the lines above and below; stage A's rule (`GlyphRule`) takes the glyphs under the hit, now with each glyph's own quad for turned text.
- MuPDF rewrites the whole content stream (operators normalized, `Td` as `TD`, resources renamed, one `q … Q`); here only the reached operations are rewritten, comments are blanked, a `Q` with nothing to restore is dropped and what the content leaves open is closed before the boxes.
- Widgets under a mark go with their fields; MuPDF keeps them (value and appearance over the black box).
- The /ActualText, /Alt and /E of property lists around removed content go, inline and named (MuPDF keeps inline ones; the preview's `inlineAlt` warned about them), and so do those of the structure element's ancestors. The element of the MCID also loses its title /T (acceptance fixes), as MuPDF's does: MuPDF drops /T, /E, /ActualText and /Alt of that element and keeps its ancestors' (measured by the acceptance review; stage B wrongly said /ActualText and /Alt only). Ancestors keep /T: a section's title names more than the redacted content.
- Every annotation a mark rect touches goes with the marks (Text notes, Highlights, FreeText, Stamps, Links, hidden ones; a Popup with its parent; Widgets with their fields); MuPDF removes only the Redact annotations and keeps the rest, their /Contents, /RC and appearances over or around the black box. So `sanitized.annots` and `kept.annots` count what is left after removal and can be lower than engine.ts's, and an annotation straddling a mark goes whole, its part outside the mark too (pdfjs issue19424.pdf: 5,362 pixels outside the marks change; MuPDF 0). Decided: an annotation's text cannot be cut to the mark, and a note under the black box describes what it hides.
- An XObject a marked page's resources list but its content never draws is kept (resources are rewritten in place, not rebuilt); when it holds a search text, the raw check finds it and apply refuses `verify-failed` ("raw check failed: a searched text is still in the marked pages' content"), where MuPDF's rebuilt resources drop it and engine.ts applies (review probe s40). Decided: fail closed; dropping it would need a private copy of shared resources and to know nothing else reaches them (Type 3 glyphs and groups without their own /Resources use the page's).
- Image objects are rewritten in place in the copy (every draw of them is under the marks, or the preview refused; `shared` now also counts draws in tiling-pattern cells, soft-mask groups (and the group itself) and Type 3 glyph procedures, an image another image takes as /SMask or /Mask, /Alternates and a page's /Thumb) and keep their names; MuPDF writes new objects under new names. Soft masks and masks are blanked too, in copies; an image left wholly blank is one pixel; a placement wholly under a mark is no longer drawn.
- A marked page's /Thumb, /PieceInfo and /Metadata, and a rewritten image's or Form's /Metadata and /PieceInfo, are dropped; MuPDF and engine.ts keep them (a thumbnail of the unredacted page, an Illustrator /Private copy of the artwork).
- Axial and radial shadings are clipped out of the marks; function and mesh shadings under a mark refuse.
- The text check reads the copy's own view and walk with the removal's rule (engine.ts: no structured-text character box meets the mark rect inset by 2 points). The pixel check takes each quad of a search mark (engine.ts the union, which a hit over two lines leaves partly unboxed).
- The raw check reads shown strings, alternate texts and comments, decoded, and also Latin-1 and UTF-16BE without its mark, a `TJ` array's strings together; engine.ts searched raw bytes (operators, names and numbers included: a search for `100` failed on `100 0 0 100 cm`, one for `e` on `/Lang (en)`).
- The 12 MiB guard measures the plaintext rewrite (engine.ts the encrypted save, a few bytes per string and stream larger). The 40-million-pixel bound applies to the images a mark touches (engine.ts: any image on a marked page), with engine.ts's message. One apply decodes at most 400 million pixels (engine.ts: no total bound).
- `validate(…, sanitized)` takes the expected encryption from the pending setting; a plain document with a pending AES-256 setting is redacted into an R6 copy that opens with its user password, as engine.ts does (the stage B note that engine.ts refused it was wrong).
- Refusals engine.ts did not have, where the marks cannot be applied exactly (`invalid`, "… so the marks cannot be applied exactly"): a removed glyph that moves the pen at size 0 in a font an ExtGState set (with a `Tf` name the pen moves at size 1); a Form whose text two placements show differently; an image or inline image that does not decode; a function or mesh shading under a mark (decided: removing the `sh` would keep its positional data in the file); a paint whose tiling pattern or soft-mask group shows text, images or such a shading, or a shading pattern of one; removed text in a Type 3 font whose glyphs draw text, images or shadings; a page written into /Kids. MuPDF applied these as it could (it keeps pattern cells, groups and glyph procedures).
- `sanitize.struct` clears and counts the alternate texts of property lists named in every page's resources and the Forms they reach (engine.ts reached them only through a structure element's /Pg, and walked everything the pages reach besides); `inlineAlt` still reports the inline ones it cannot clear.
- Sanitize clears more than redact.ts (acceptance fixes; counts unchanged): `fields` also removes /RV (the rich-text value) and /I (a choice's selected indices, which name the choice with /V gone; /Opt, the form's options, stays); `layers` also removes the /Name of /D and of each /Configs entry; `links` removes /URI, /F, /Win, /Mac and /Unix from each Link's action and every action its /Next chain reaches (direct or by reference, 4,096 action objects and 32 levels at most).
- Every new stream is Flate-compressed: redact.pdf's copy is 16,888 bytes (engine.ts 34,973).
- The copy is reopened for its checks as the app opens documents (`open_options`): a stream naming an external file keeps its bytes and /F, /FFilter and /FDecodeParms (app-corpus-open.md), and the copy's view reads it from its own bytes (a plain copy without those keys: hayro refuses /F). Before acceptance round 2 any copy keeping one refused `verify-failed` ("output is not one compact revision"); engine.ts applies.
- The copy's view keeps the session view's resource budget (`view_cost`): past it apply refuses `too-large` ("the pages' resources are larger than the view's limit, so the copy cannot be checked"), as the session's own view refuses such a document. engine.ts applies it, slowly (review probe p39: 1,200 pages sharing 4,000 fonts, 912 MB).

### Gaps

- A tiling-pattern cell or soft-mask group of paths and axial or radial shadings that a redacted draw paints with is kept as it is (a texture, a gradient; the paint under the mark is cut): a cell or group that draws a vector shape (a logo used as a mask) keeps that shape in the file. Type 3 glyph shapes stay with the font.
- `shared` counts what patterns, groups and glyph procedures can draw from any resources a page reaches (whether or not a paint uses them), so it may refuse an object that such content alone could draw.
- The raw check, as engine.ts's, reads page content and Forms, not the appearances of annotations no mark touches.
- Sanitize surfaces both engines keep (parity with redact.ts, review probe p6): embedded files reached through the catalog's or a page's /AF (`attachments` clears /Names /EmbeddedFiles and FileAttachment annotations, and counts those only); /JS of link actions; an OCG's /Usage (creator, user names); and the targets of actions other than Links' (/OpenAction, /AA).
- Text the search does not find (the view's) is not marked; when the raw check finds the search text left in the content, apply refuses.
- A marked page whose /Contents include a stream encoded with an image filter refuses the preview and apply (`invalid`, "stream N cannot be decoded"): the walk reads every element. The view and MuPDF leave such a stream out (so redact-search finds the page's other text) and engine.ts applies (review probe p35). Fail closed.
- Outside this piece: the view's text reader (`stext.rs` `Scan::content`) finds an MCID's structure element with `parents.iter().nth(mcid)`, resolving every element before it, so a page with n MCIDs reads about n²/2 objects (6,000 MCIDs: 8.7 s natively in redact-apply's text check; text extraction and redact-search pay it too).

## redact-inspect (redesign, October 18)

- `redact-inspect {regions: [{page, rect}]}` (`src/app/redact/inspect.rs`, op in `ops/redact.rs`): per box, what redact-apply would remove under it, for the window's "show what's underneath" tint. Read only; allowed on signed documents (the UI keeps boxes in memory there), refused on locked ones. Rects are redact-add's (y-down page space, clamped, at least 1 by 1 pt); bad regions refuse `invalid` with detail `regions`, more than 2,000 `too-many`.
- One walk per page (`Detail::Full`, `Limits::DEFAULT`) with one `GlyphRule` for all the page's boxes (one query budget, `MAX_QUERY_VISITS`); a removed glyph is attributed to each box whose own rule takes it (candidates from the boxes' index; attribution work bounded by the same constant). Glyphs: `removes_glyph` and, whole, unmeasured runs that `reaches`; adjacent upright glyphs of one show joined into one quad. Text: the view's characters (unclipped) whose centre lies in a removed glyph's quad (point in convex quad, so turned or skewed text claims no neighbour), structured text read once per page. Paths (painted), shadings and pictures a box touches: the part under the box, `whole` where apply removes the whole draw (inside the box, a singular matrix, an image whose unit square cannot be inverted). Annotations and fields a box touches (kind = subtype lower-cased, a field's full name or a comment's start as label); only the touched widget goes, a field's other widgets stay (as apply).
- Bounded reply: at most 10,000 glyph quads, shapes and pictures in all (`MAX_ITEMS`); past it a box lists one quad per show with removed glyphs, then only its own rect, and says `truncated`; text at most 10,000 characters per box. Every number is finite (a glyph the walk could not place is its box cut to the page, else the page box); numbers are rounded to 0.01.
- `needsFlatten` says a box covers content apply can only flatten (R4).

## The redesign: draw a box, everything inside is gone (October 18-19)

Plan: `docs/rust-and-ux-plan.md` §2.4 (owner decisions D4, D5). Tests: `tests/app_redact_boxes.rs`; MuPDF proof: `oracle/compare-app-redact.mjs`, `reference/app-redact-20261019/summary.json`.

- **R1, glyphs.** See "Region mapping" above: area marks (every mark without the search tag) take every glyph their box meets less 0.5 pt; search marks keep the central-half rule; glyphs narrower than 0.1 em reach 0.5 em either way from the pen.
- **R6, regions.** `redact-apply {regions: [{page, rect}], sanitize, flatten?, unsign?}`: boxes in redact-add's space, clamped the same way, held in memory as area marks (`Mark::area`) and applied with the document's own marks; nothing is written to the session. Bad regions refuse `invalid` with detail `regions`; past 2,000 boxes `too-many`. The reply adds `flattened` and `unsigned`.
- **redact-inspect.** Its own section above.
- **R5, raw check for area boxes** (`verify::box_runs`, `RunCounter`, `visible_runs`, `hidden_runs`). Before apply, the source view's characters whose centres lie in an area mark give runs: each maximal stretch of a line (at least 4 characters besides white space) and each of its words of 6 or more characters; at most 4,096 runs of 256 characters. After apply, white space aside: a run's occurrences in the marked pages' content (strings shown per operator, alternate texts, comments; decoded as text strings or UTF-16BE) may not exceed what those pages of the copy show; with what the copy holds outside page content (strings under /Contents, /RC, /V, /DV, /TU, /ActualText, /Alt, /E, /Title, /Subject, /Author, /Keywords in any object, every /Info string, XMP text without markup) they may not exceed what every page shows. Failure: `verify-failed`, detail `raw`, `pages`; the run is never named. Decided: "anywhere in the copy" counts against what the copy still shows, so a word boxed once and printed elsewhere does not block apply. Counting is one pass per text (runs indexed by their first 4 characters).
- **R2, image colours** (`images::black`, `prepare`). Blanked pixels take the box's black through the image's /Decode: Gray and RGB 0, CMYK 0 0 0 1, Lab L 0 (a, b 0 within /Range), ICCBased by its components, Separation and DeviceN full colorant (the darkest without evaluating the tint transform). Indexed: the darkest entry when every RGB channel is at most 0.15; otherwise black joins the palette (the samples widened to 8 bits when the palette is full at their depth); a full 8-bit palette without a colour-key mask becomes 8-bit DeviceRGB. Inline images keep their dictionary (the darkest entry). Rows and columns a box overlaps by any sliver are blanked (rounded outward).
- **R3, shared graphics** (`redact::private`). When the preview's `shared` is not empty, every XObject placement on a marked page whose drawing a box touches (an image a box touches; a Form with something a box touches inside, at any depth; a shading in a Form without a usable /BBox) gets its own copy under a new name before apply plans: the page gets one new content stream and its own resources, a copied Form names its children's copies, and names no longer drawn leave those resources (the raw checks read what the marked pages' resources reach). Originals stay for every other placement. An image the view decodes is decoded through its original's number. At most 65,536 copies and 256 MiB per apply (`too-large`). Still shared after the copies: `shared-content` (safety net). Gap: a Type 3 font or pattern without its own /Resources that names a page XObject the copy dropped would lose it.
- **R4, flatten** (`apply::pages_to_flatten`, `apply::flatten`). Content only flattening can redact (above) refuses `needs-flatten` with every such page (0-based, each planned on its own). With the page in `flatten`, the view draws it without annotations at 200 dpi (fewer past 16,777,216 pixels; a letter page is 3.7 million), every pixel a box touches is burnt black, and the content becomes that one picture over the page box (through the page space, so /Rotate and crop boxes hold); its structure elements lose their alternate texts, its derived copies go, the annotations its marks touch go with the marks. The current layer visibility is what is drawn (decided). A listed page without marks is left alone; a bad page refuses `invalid` with detail `flatten`. The text, pixel and raw checks run as for any page. redact-inspect's `needsFlatten` comes from the same planning.
- **D5, signed files** (`sanitize::remove_signatures`). A signed or certified document refuses `signed`; with `unsign: true` (the edit permission still checked) the copy loses every signed signature field (own /FT /Sig with /V) with what is under it (from /Fields, the pages and every reference, so the signature dictionaries are not written), the catalog's /Perms and the form's /SigFlags; unsigned signature fields stay. The session's document stays signed. redact-add still refuses on a signed file, so the window sends its boxes as `regions`.
- **Measured** (MuPDF oracle, `reference/app-redact-20261019`): one box over the whole of page 1 of `fixtures/redact.pdf`: MuPDF reads no secret on page 1 and finds it in one object, the Form page 2 draws outside any box (engine.ts refuses `shared-content`); a box over each page: in no object, both engines. The earlier scenarios are as in `app-redact-20261015`.
