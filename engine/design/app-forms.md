# Forms op group (fields, field-set, field-add, field-props, field-delete)

October 15, 2026. Port of engine.ts's form ops onto `AppSession`. Code: `src/app/forms/` (model, appearance synthesis, writing, the view's synthesized appearances) and `src/app/ops/fields.rs` (the five ops). Tests: `tests/app_forms.rs`. Evidence: `reference/app-forms-20261015/`.

## Design

- **Model** (`forms/mod.rs`). Widgets are the /Subtype /Widget dictionaries of the page's /Annots, in order, each object once. Attributes are read as MuPDF's accessors read them: /FT, /Ff, /V, /MaxLen, /Opt, /TU, /T inherited along /Parent (`pagetree::inherited`, 64 levels); full names joined over the /T chain (empty parts skipped, empty on a /Parent cycle); `widget_type` is `pdf_field_type` (unknown /FT is a push button). The engine.ts walks bounded at 8 nodes (`fieldValueName`, `fullFieldName`, `widgetScripted`) keep their bound.
- **Ids.** A widget's id is its object number. A widget written directly into /Annots lists as id 0, as engine.ts reports it, and cannot be filled, changed or deleted (`stale-target`).
- **Page space.** `PageSpace` is MuPDF's page transform (CropBox within MediaBox, UserUnit, snapped /Rotate) in single precision, as `pdf_page_transform`, `fz_transform_rect` and `fz_invert_matrix` compute it and as `info::page_size` reports the page: its bounds are exactly the page size the app is sent, so a full-page rect, or a listed rect sent back, is on the page. `fields` reports /Rect mapped into page space (single-precision values, as MuPDF's); field-add and field-props write the request rect (read as single precision) mapped back (`pdf_set_annot_rect`), each number the shortest decimal of its single-precision value; integral values are written as integers.
- **Journal.** Every write goes through `AppSession::mutate`'s `Transaction` (`Edit` and `Place` for values inside objects): one step per op, named `fill field`, `add field`, `change field` or `delete field`. Validation comes first and writes nothing, in engine.ts's order with its messages, reasons and details; a refusal never changes the journal. Batch modes work through `mutate` (edit-batch's plan runs the checks only). `fields::add_field(session, request, value)` is engine.ts `addField` with clip-paste's value override.
- **Appearances** (`forms/appearance.rs`). MuPDF 1.28's synthesis (`pdf_write_tx_widget_appearance`, the check box, radio and push button writers), measured on 102 recorded cases (`reference/app-forms-20261015/synthesis.json`, `tools/app-forms-oracle.mjs --synthesis`): every stream has MuPDF's operators, strings and numbers (to single precision) except the 16 deliberate differences below, asserted by `synthesis_matches_mupdfs_recorded_streams`; `fallback_fonts_match_mupdfs` compares the fallback font dictionaries. Text: border width from /BS /W or /Border (default 1), /MK /BG and /BC, clip inside the border, padding twice the border, baseline 0.8 and line height 1.116 of the size, DA size 0 meaning the height shrunk to fit the width (12 pt for multiline), quadding from /Q or /AcroForm /Q, multiline before comb (MuPDF's order), comb cells across the whole width with dividers when /MK /BC is set and /MaxLen passes 1, a comb field without a positive /MaxLen drawn as one line without padding, /MK /R turning the form. Check boxes draw ZapfDingbats `3` at the box size; radio buttons MuPDF's circles. Numbers are printed as MuPDF prints them (shortest single-precision digits, no exponent, `.5`).
- **Text outside WinAnsi** (`forms/scripts.rs`), as MuPDF's writer splits it into runs: Latin in the DA font (WinAnsi); Greek in ISO 8859-7 and Cyrillic in KOI8-U, each a new simple font of the DA font's base 14 family named `<DA>GRK` or `<DA>CYR`, with MuPDF's WinAnsi-based /Differences (its glyph names) and /Widths (the URW advances of those glyphs, FirstChar 32 to the last drawn code); Han and Bopomofo in Song, Mincho, Batang or Ming by the widget's inherited /Lang, else the catalog's (MuPDF's `fz_text_language_from_string`: none, zh-Hans or zh-CN give Song, other Chinese Ming, ko Batang, anything else Mincho), kana in Mincho, Hangul in Batang, as UTF-16 hex through MuPDF's Type0 font (`Uni*-UTF16-H`, a CIDFontType0 and its fixed descriptor), every character 1000 wide. A Common character takes the font of the script around it; at a line's start (and the text's) that is the script of the next scripted character. Runs re-select their font at each line.
- **Lossy text.** A character no fallback encodes (Latin Extended such as Polish, Hebrew, Arabic, arrows, combining marks: where MuPDF switches to embedding a font program) is drawn as `?` and the synthesis is lossy (`Synth::lossy`). The view draws it so; a write never stores it: `refresh` removes the widget's /AP and sets /AcroForm /NeedAppearances true, so viewers draw the value themselves (undo restores both).
- `generated_appearance.rs` (the preview session's display-only appearances) is not used: its layout approximates MuPDF's where this module reproduces it; the font modules (`builtin_metrics`, `simple_encoding`) give the advances and WinAnsi codes.
- **Fonts.** Text is WinAnsi bytes. The DA font is the /DR font when it is a simple WinAnsi font (Type1, TrueType, MMType1), measured with its /Widths over its base 14 family's advances; otherwise a new base 14 font of its family (Helvetica when /DR lacks the name) under the DA's name, one object per op. ZapfDingbats is /DR's when /DR has it without an /Encoding, else a new one without /Encoding.
- **Writing** (`forms/write.rs`). A `Sink` allocates streams and objects and keeps one font object per font (base 14, fallback or CID) for its operation: `EditSink` on any `Edit` target (`TxSink` is the journaled `Transaction`'s; a redaction's or batch file's scratch `Builder` works the same), the view's in its private update. `refresh` (generic over `Edit`) replaces a widget's /AP with new objects and gives a button a name /AS (Off) when it has none; the replaced streams become unreachable and leave with the next save. `ops::fields::remove_widget` (generic over `Edit`) is field-delete's removal from /Annots, /Fields and a parent's /Kids, for redaction and clipboard ops to share.
- **When appearances are written.** field-set of a text or choice field redraws every widget of the field (the node with /T and every widget below it, 4,096 at most). field-add draws every new widget but a signature's. field-props redraws when rect, maxLen, multiline, edit or options change, and drops a signature field's /AP instead. A fill of a check box or radio button writes /V and /AS only, as MuPDF does.
- **View** (`forms/view.rs`, hooked into `AppDocument::view`). As MuPDF does for display only, a widget without /AP /N, and every widget when /AcroForm /NeedAppearances is true, is drawn with a synthesized appearance: the widget redefined with /AP (and /AS when it has none) in the view's private update, the streams and fonts new objects numbered from the allocation floor. `overlay::join_redefined` writes new streams for this. Saves and `snapshot_bytes` never contain them. Signature fields, signed or not, and widgets without /FT keep what they have. Bounded at 20,000 widgets and 32 MiB of content per view; a drawn value is cut at 65,536 characters and multiline lines stop below the box.
- **Parser depth.** The app's parse limits allow 256 nested arrays and dictionaries (was 64): MuPDF reads a structure tree 81 levels deep (p6-tabs "debt1 row 4"). The app's write limits (`overlay::limits`: save, the render overlay, the plaintext rewrite of an encrypted base, page extraction) take the same depth, so whatever opens can be edited, drawn and saved (`deep_documents_open_edit_render_and_save`, 255 levels; on wasm32 too). The view's parser (hayro) refuses objects nested past 64 levels, and the whole document when the catalog is one: `app::view_depth` redefines such an object, its containers past 64 levels cut to null, in the view's private update only (non-stream objects; saves keep the depth).
- **Bounds.** Field tree walks are bounded as engine.ts bounds them (4,096 nodes, depth 8); the name check (field-add, field-props renames, signature auto-names) walks each node's /Kids one at a time, never visits past depth 8, and keeps only names of at most 128 UTF-16 units (no longer one can equal a request; a /T over 516 bytes is not decoded), so its work and memory are bounded by the 4,096 visits whatever the /T lengths and /Kids sizes. Fills of a radio group visit 4,096 nodes at most; structure order stops at 50,000 nodes or 64 levels. Synthesis reads a value only up to the 65,536 characters it draws (`value_prefix`, `text_prefix`), lays out 65,536 lines at most, reads a list box's options from /TI only as far as lines can show, its /V once into a set (65,536 entries at most), and a combo box's first 65,536 options; a /DA over 4 KiB reads as empty. The view also stops past 64 MiB of /DA, value and option bytes read (`view::MAX_WORK`). The `fields` listing cuts values and defaults at 65,536 characters (no fill can write one back: `MAX_NOTE_CHARS`), names, labels, tooltips, on states and options at 4,096 characters, options at 4,096, and refuses a page whose item strings pass 32 MiB (`too-large`). No op code panics on hostile input (`hostile_fields_never_panic`: cycles in /Parent and /Kids, odd /DA, /Ff, /MaxLen, /Rect and /MK values; `field_name_walks_stay_bounded`, `the_view_bounds_its_synthesis_work`, `the_listing_cuts_long_strings_and_refuses_huge_pages`).

## Measurements

- Review fixes, October 15: write depth matched to the parser's; single-precision page space; /Ff read through references; MuPDF's comb order; bounded name walk, synthesis reads and listing; MuPDF's Greek, Cyrillic and CID fallback fonts, lossy text left to the viewer; `EditSink` and `remove_widget` generic over `Edit`. 102 synthesis cases (43 new: comb variants, every fallback font and family, language tags, run splitting, line contexts, lossy text).
- `tools/app-forms-diff.mjs` (`reference/app-forms-20261015/differential.json`): `fields` on all 54 fixtures (30 pages each at most) gives the same items as engine.ts on MuPDF, compared as values; 11 op sequences (`sequences.json`: adds of each kind with labels, flags, defaults and edit combos, fills, undo and redo, renames, props, deletes, refusals, malformed requests, signed, certified, XFA, locked and permission-limited files, the rotated and cropped pages of p3/form-design.pdf) give the same replies, errors included, and the same listing after every step, object numbers aside (MuPDF allocates more objects).
- Widget pixels (`reference/app-forms-20261015/summary.json`, render op at scale 2, 9 scenarios, 60 widgets). Rust (hayro) against engine.ts (MuPDF), exact / within 16 levels: text 93.45 / 96.87%, checkbox 93.85 / 96.97%, combo 98.27 / 98.92%, radio 83.60 / 89.33%, list 79.61 / 80.27%, signature 97.14 / 97.19%, text outside WinAnsi (`text-intl`: Cyrillic, Greek, CJK, Korean, mixed, Polish) 96.12 / 96.51%. The remainder is the renderers (glyph programs, anti-aliasing of curves and strokes; hayro draws no glyphs for the fallback fonts) plus the differences below. The two engines' saved files drawn by MuPDF alone (the appearance streams, without the renderer difference): checkbox, combo, radio, signature and text-intl 100% (the Polish field too: MuPDF redraws Rust's NeedAppearances file as engine.ts's), text 99.44% (the two props fields where engine.ts falls back to its single-line stream), list 80.47% (the selection highlight).
- App tests on Rust (`node tests/run-rust.mjs`): 162 → 213 passing, no test that passed before fails.

## Differences from MuPDF and engine.ts

- A widget written directly into /Annots lists with id 0 but cannot be filled, changed or deleted; engine.ts would act on the first such widget for id 0.
- The listing names each widget object once; MuPDF lists an object /Annots repeats once per entry.
- A fill sets the wanted state instead of toggling: a check box whose /V says on but whose /AS is missing turns off on `false` (MuPDF's toggle reads only /AS and would turn it on).
- A choice fill writes /V on the field (the nearest node with /T), like a text fill, and removes any /V on the nodes between it and the widget; MuPDF writes /V on the widget dictionary. A text or choice fill redraws every widget of the field, not only the one filled (engine.ts calls `update()` on one widget).
- field-props redraws with the full synthesis whatever changed; engine.ts's `refreshAppearance` falls back to its own single-line stream when no `setRect` dirtied the widget (a maxLen, multiline, options or edit change without a rect), so a multiline field changed there is drawn on one line until its next fill.
- New streams replace a widget's /AP; MuPDF rewrites its own earlier appearance stream in place. Text appearances name the /DR font object; MuPDF adds a separate Helvetica font object.
- field-delete also drops every other reference to the deleted widget, and to a radio group its last kid emptied (structure OBJRs, another page's /Annots), so a save never writes them; MuPDF keeps them reachable through the structure tree. Deleting the kid the group's /V names sets /V to Off (engine.ts leaves /V naming a kid that is gone). A missing /AcroForm /Fields is left alone (engine.ts throws a TypeError and abandons).
- field-add replaces a non-array /AcroForm /Fields with a new array (engine.ts fails pushing onto it). A page dictionary written directly into /Kids is refused with a plain error.
- Password fields draw one `*` per character; MuPDF draws the value in clear.
- A combo box draws the display text of the selected option (/Opt pairs); MuPDF draws the export value.
- A list box highlights its selected options (Acrobat's colour, behind each selected option's lines); MuPDF draws no selection.
- Check marks and radio dots take the DA colour, and a one-character /MK /CA chooses the ZapfDingbats mark (a radio's `l` keeps MuPDF's dot); MuPDF always draws a black `3` and a black dot.
- The push button background fills the whole box; MuPDF 1.28 writes `0 0 0 w re` and fills nothing. A push button's automatic size is at least 1 pt.
- A DA font /DR lacks keeps its name in the appearance's resources, and a /DR font named other than Helv, TiRo, Cour, Symb or ZaDb (HeBo) draws as /DR has it; MuPDF renames both Helv (Helvetica). The fallback fonts follow: `<DA>CYR` of the /DR font's family (MuPDF: HelvCYR). Bytes outside printable ASCII are written as octal escapes.
- A Common character the current script's font lacks is drawn in the first of the WinAnsi, KOI8-U and ISO 8859-7 fonts that has it (`Привет €`: € in Helv; `a≤b`: ≤ in HelvCYR); MuPDF draws a bullet (`·`) in Helv. In a CJK run every Common character, any plane, is drawn in the CID font as UTF-16 (`日→`, `カナー`, `日。`); MuPDF embeds a font for those not in its 8-bit encodings, and writes a code point past the BMP as broken hex (`<20000>`), not a surrogate pair.
- Lossy text (no fallback has a character: Polish, Czech, Hebrew, Arabic, arrows, combining marks) writes no appearance and sets /AcroForm /NeedAppearances; MuPDF embeds Nimbus Sans or Droid Sans Fallback subsets through its rich-text writer. The view draws `?` for such characters until the viewer redraws them.
- The view (hayro) has no Cyrillic or Greek glyphs in its standard fonts and no CID fonts, so it draws nothing for the fallback fonts' runs; MuPDF draws them with its own fonts. A render gap for the view group (the URW fonts the engine carries have the glyphs); saved files are MuPDF's.
- The Times fallback fonts' /Widths take the AFM advance where MuPDF's CFF one differs by a unit (Ш, Щ: 1009 against 1008).
- Numbers are computed in double precision and printed at single precision, so a few last digits differ from MuPDF's single-precision arithmetic (`3.2` for `3.1999994`).
- The view draws nothing for an unsigned signature field without /AP; MuPDF draws its "SIGN" marker (the known view difference).
- A /MK /R that is not a multiple of 90 is read as 0.
