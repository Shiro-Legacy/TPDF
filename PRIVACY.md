# Privacy Policy

*Tandem PDF. Last updated October 28, 2026.*

Tandem PDF is a local, offline PDF editor. It does not collect, send or sell any information about you
or your documents.

## What Tandem PDF does not do

- It does not send your documents, or anything about them, anywhere. PDFs are opened, edited, signed,
  redacted and saved on your computer.
- It has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, tracking or telemetry.
- It contains no networking code and never connects to the internet. The Mac App Store version
  runs in Apple's App Sandbox. It does carry the sandbox's network permission, because the macOS
  web engine that draws Tandem PDF's window needs that permission to start, but Tandem PDF never
  uses it.

## What stays on your computer

Tandem PDF keeps a few small files for its own use. They live in the app's folder on your computer and
are never sent anywhere:

- **Recent files:** the list of PDFs you opened recently. In the Mac App Store version, this includes
  macOS bookmarks that let those files reopen after a restart. File ▸ Open Recent ▸ Clear Menu removes
  them.
- **Unsaved changes:** a recovery copy of edits you have not saved yet, so they survive a crash. It is
  removed when you save or discard the changes.
- **Your drawn signature and trusted certificates**, if you choose to save them.
- **Window size and display settings.**

Deleting the app's folder removes all of them. On a Mac, the App Store version keeps them in
`~/Library/Containers/com.shiro-legacy.tandem-pdf`.

## Links

If a document contains a web or email link and you choose to open it, Tandem PDF first asks you, then
hands the link to your browser or mail app. What happens after that is up to that app and the website.

## Crash reports

If you have allowed it in your system settings, Apple may share crash reports with the developer. These
reports come from Apple, not from Tandem PDF, and contain technical details about the crash, not your
documents.

## Children

Tandem PDF collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
